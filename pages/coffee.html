Three roasts. Three ways to experience Uganda.

Every Nganzi roast begins with carefully sourced Ugandan coffee. The roasting profile shapes the aroma, sweetness, body, and finish you meet in the cup.

Bright & expressive

Light Roast

A lively roast that preserves the bean’s floral character and fruit-forward acidity. Best for slow mornings, pour-over rituals, and drinkers who enjoy a delicate, layered cup.

Tasting Notes

  • Honey
  • Red berry
  • Jasmine

Balanced & aromatic

Medium Roast

Our most versatile roast balances caramel sweetness, citrus brightness, and a clean cocoa finish. Smooth enough for everyday brewing and expressive enough to drink black.

Tasting Notes

  • Caramel
  • Orange zest
  • Cocoa
Ask about Medium Roast

Bold & full-bodied

Dark Roast

A deep, slow roast with a rich body and lingering smoky finish. It stands up beautifully to milk and delivers the intensity espresso and French press drinkers love.

Tasting Notes

  • Dark chocolate
  • Molasses
  • Toasted almond
